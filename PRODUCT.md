# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

macos

## Users

Primary audience is students and researchers doing deep reading with highlights and notes. Secondary audience is general readers who want a calm, native ebook and PDF experience on the Mac.

## Product Purpose

Leaf helps people read deeply and capture lasting highlights and notes without leaving the page, while managing a personal library and picking up where they left off. When chrome space is scarce, the three-pane balance stays sacred: library, reader, and notebook.

## Positioning

Genuinely native macOS reading and study — no Electron or web shell, system feel throughout.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, scholarly. Quiet confidence; tools for attention and study without dashboard noise. Voice matches Apple Books and Preview more than productivity SaaS.

## Anti-references

- Electron and web-shell readers (Kindle desktop, Calibre-like density, browser chrome)
- Loud SaaS dashboards — purple accents, card grids, badge clutter
- Over-decorated "aesthetic" reading apps — cream-paper clichés, sticker UI

## Design Principles

- **Native first** — look and behave like a Mac app, not a website in a window
- **Protect attention** — chrome serves the page; never compete with the text
- **Three panes, equal dignity** — library, reader, and notebook stay in balance
- **Scholarly calm** — tools for study without dashboard noise
- **System literacy** — menus, shortcuts, inspectors, and materials users already know

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ship with what Apple frameworks provide by default. No accessibility bar beyond VoiceOver, keyboard, and system settings the platform already exposes.
