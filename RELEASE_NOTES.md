# Leaf Native 1.3.0

A clearer research companion for reading and following sources.

## What's new

- Paper titles now open their DOI pages. Each result also offers **Open paper** and **Copy citation**, plus **Open PDF** when OpenAlex provides a direct PDF link.
- Paper results show matching abstract terms when available, so you can see why a result may be relevant before opening it. Bibliographic metadata is checked with Crossref; this does not verify a paper's findings.
- Paper searches that refer to "this part," page, section, or chapter now use the reading passage as their search context.
- Document citations and external research links sit in separate, expandable source sections. Document citations still jump back to the passage in your book.
- Answers have clearer headings and nested lists. Longer responses start with a brief takeaway when the model follows the new format guidance.
- The companion follows the Mac's light or dark appearance, and the model picker takes less room in the pane.

## Downloads

- **DMG** — drag Leaf Native into Applications
- **ZIP** — portable app bundle
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** — checksums for both downloads

Leaf Native requires macOS 15 or later (macOS 26 for the on-device Apple Intelligence provider).

## First launch

This build is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, right-click Leaf Native in Applications and choose **Open**, or run:

```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Leaf Native.app"
```
